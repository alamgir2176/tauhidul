Md Tauhidul Islam

Cherrapunji, Meghalaya

I’m Tauhidul. I come from the Barishal region in southern Bangladesh, a land of rivers and wide green fields, and it’s still the place I think of as home.

From there I went to Jahangirnagar University to study philosophy, and I stayed on for my master’s too. During those years I read Western and Indian thought, Bengali philosophy, and logic. Whatever I was reading, what held me was how gently philosophy asks its questions: how the mind works, and how a word comes to mean something.

Lately those questions have turned toward the tools around us. I like trying out new things, such as AI tools and simple websites, and I wonder how we might live well alongside them.

Not everything is thought, though. I have spent time with student volunteer groups, welcoming new students and helping where I could, and I like to wander and see how people live in other places. And I always come back to Bengali novels. I keep a small collection of them.
