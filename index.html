I’m Tauhidul, a philosophy graduate from Bangladesh. I studied Philosophy at Jahangirnagar University, where I completed both my BA and MA, and I keep coming back to the same few questions: how the mind works, how words come to mean something, and what technology asks of us ethically.

My reading moves between Western and Indian philosophy, the philosophy of Bengal, logic, and the ethics of technology. I also like to make things. I experiment with AI tools, no-code platforms and WordPress, and I’ve followed courses such as the Elements of AI to understand how these tools actually work.

Beyond the classroom, I spent about three years with student volunteer groups, handing out winter clothes to 300 people, welcoming new students, and running helpdesks for admission-test candidates. I donated blood, helped connect people in need with free blood, and performed on stage with Jahangirnagar Theatre.

I’m from the Barishal region in southern Bangladesh. I collect Bengali literature, mostly novels, and I travel whenever I can, to see how people live in other places. At university I was awarded a supplementary scholarship twice, in my first and third years.
