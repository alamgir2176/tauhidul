About

I’m Tauhidul. I’ve just finished my BA and MA in Philosophy at Jahangirnagar University, and I’m now looking for a full-time job.

Philosophy trained me to break a problem into parts, test an argument for weak spots, and explain a hard idea plainly. My coursework covered symbolic logic, decision theory and research methodology, and my master’s courses leaned toward the ethics of technology, cyber ethics, and the philosophy of mind and language.

I also like making things with modern tools. I use Canva, WordPress and no-code platforms, I build with AI assistance, and I’ve completed short courses on generative AI, the basics of AI, digital marketing and blockchain.
