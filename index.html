Cherrapunji, Meghalaya

I’m Tauhidul. I come from the Barishal region in southern Bangladesh, a land of rivers and wide green fields, and it is still the place I think of as home.

From there I went to Jahangirnagar University to study philosophy, and I stayed on through my bachelor’s and master’s. What held me was how gently philosophy asks its questions: how the mind works, and how a word comes to mean something.

Lately those questions have turned toward the tools around us. I like trying out new things, such as AI tools and simple websites, and wondering how we might live well beside them and use them with care. Reading helps me think it through: Western and Indian thought, the philosophy of Bengal, and logic for the clarity it brings.

Not everything is thought, though. I have spent time with student volunteer groups, welcoming new students and helping where I could, and I like to wander and see how people live in other places. And I always come back to a Bengali novel, of which I keep a small collection.
